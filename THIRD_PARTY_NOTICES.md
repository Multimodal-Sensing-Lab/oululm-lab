# Sources, data and licensing

- **Original toolkit:** the repository MIT license covers original code, explanations, generated diagrams, controlled classroom stories, word corpus, fictional handbook and authored Q&A examples, and our tiny model weights. No downloaded pretrained model weights are included.
- **Supplied synthetic CSVs:** `learning/llm-workshop-001/data/external/` contains biology, finance and email corpora. The supplied provenance records Claude Opus 5-assisted deterministic generator scripts, September 2026. The CSVs retain the documented **CC0 1.0 Universal** dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Generator scripts are MIT. Attribution is retained even where CC0 does not require it.
- **Optional downloaded data:** loaders can download FinancialPhraseBank (the referenced dataset card lists CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), PubMedQA (MIT in its dataset card) and TinyStories (CDLA-Sharing 1.0). These datasets and their caches are **not bundled** in this MIT toolkit. Their original terms apply when downloaded. Source IDs and links are in the data README.
- **Reference illustrations:** Jay Alammar's original illustrations use CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. This public package links to the source instead of redistributing those images. Original workshop SVG diagrams are included under MIT.
- **Acknowledged teaching resources:** OpinTori (Constantino Alvarez, 2026), the supplied scoping-review teaching presentation, Stanford CS324, Transformer Explainer, Brendan Bycroft's visualization, 3Blue1Brown, Jay Alammar and David Epstein's Q/K/V article informed preparation. References remain linked. Third-party slide decks, articles, videos and code are not copied into this package.
- **Dependencies:** PyTorch, NumPy, Matplotlib, scikit-learn, PyYAML and optional Hugging Face packages are installed separately and retain their respective licenses.
