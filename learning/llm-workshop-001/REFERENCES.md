# Open the Model / OuluLLM Lab — References

Consolidated bibliography for the presentation (through v6), its speaking notes, the character and word browser workshops, and the Python/data examples. Compiled 21 September 2026.

Entries give authors or responsible organizations, year (where established), title, publication or resource type, and a persistent identifier or source link. This is a readable bibliography, not a claim of strict APA formatting. **n.d.** means a publication date was not established. Living documentation may change. Large-team reports use team attribution, with their complete author lists available in the linked primary records. Dates of preprints and conference publication can differ.

Personal bookmarks added only to notes_for_me (for example AirLLM) are not presented as sources used to build the workshop. Local data and teaching materials are distinguished from published evidence.
